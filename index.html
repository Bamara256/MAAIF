About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 brings together farmers, exporters, researchers, buyers and policy makers for three days of learning, trade and networking. It is organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries to showcase the strength of Uganda's coffee sector.

Visitors can attend expert talks, meet exhibitors from across the coffee value chain and explore new markets and technologies. Whether you grow, roast, trade or simply love coffee, the Expo is the place to be.
